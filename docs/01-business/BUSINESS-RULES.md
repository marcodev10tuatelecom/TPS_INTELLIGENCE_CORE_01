# Master Business Rules

Initial rules:
- BRULE-001: every real-world object has one canonical identity unless evidence proves distinct identities.
- BRULE-002: roles are relationships/context, not duplicate people.
- BRULE-003: local programming may override network programming only within authorized scope and window.
- BRULE-004: expired or territorially invalid rights block protected use.
- BRULE-005: commercial frequency caps and competitor conflicts are deterministic constraints.
- BRULE-006: historically significant state is ended/superseded, not silently overwritten.
- BRULE-007: AI inference is distinguishable from verified fact.
- BRULE-008: an operational decision requiring legal/commercial/rights authority must pass policy validation.
- BRULE-009: media bytes may live outside Oracle, but canonical identity/hash/location/lineage/rights remain governed by Oracle.
- BRULE-010: every material AI decision is explainable to source entities, relations, evidence and policy result.
