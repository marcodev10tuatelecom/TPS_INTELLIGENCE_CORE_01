# Business Analysis

## Business problem
Tech Pro Solutions operates a media ecosystem where the same people, content, brands, rights, campaigns, audiences and infrastructure participate across multiple radio and TV stations. Independent per-application databases create duplication, inconsistent schedules, weak provenance and inability to reason across the network.

## Desired business state
One corporate intelligence core provides canonical identity and knowledge while allowing each station, affiliate, repeater and regional operation to keep authorized local programming and context.

## Business capabilities
- network/station/channel topology;
- 24x7 program scheduling and continuity;
- shared content libraries with station/region-specific rules;
- music/video/program metadata and lineage;
- advertiser/campaign/inventory/placement management;
- rights/territory/time-window enforcement;
- audience segmentation and analytics;
- editorial/news provenance;
- AI-assisted programming, curation and analysis;
- operational event history and explainability.

## Value
Reduce duplicated facts, increase automation, enable network-wide intelligence, preserve regional flexibility, improve auditability, create AI-ready knowledge and make applications thinner consumers of canonical services.

## Critical risk
An intelligent database must not allow probabilistic output to override legal, rights, commercial or operational constraints. Deterministic policy remains final authority for protected actions.
