# Stakeholders and Actors

Actors include corporate administrators, network directors, station managers, program directors, music directors, presenters, journalists, producers, commercial teams, advertisers, rights/compliance staff, audience analysts, application services, ingestion services, auditors and governed AI agents.

Each actor is modeled separately from its authorization role. A human or system has one canonical entity identity and receives roles/relations/privileges appropriate to context.

No AI agent is represented as a human user. AI entities have explicit agent/model/tool identities and separate audit semantics.
