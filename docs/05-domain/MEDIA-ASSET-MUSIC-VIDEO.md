# Media Asset, Music and Video Domain

Distinguish intellectual/content identity from physical/digital renditions.

Entities include SONG, RECORDING, ALBUM, VIDEO_WORK, EPISODE, PROGRAM, COMMERCIAL, JINGLE, PROMO, IMAGE and MEDIA_ASSET.

MEDIA_ASSET stores governed metadata: content linkage, SHA-256, MIME, codec, bitrate, dimensions, duration, size, storage URI/reference, lifecycle, ingest source and rights references.

Lineage relations include MASTER_OF, PROXY_OF, TRANSCODED_FROM, AUDIO_OF, VIDEO_OF, THUMBNAIL_OF, SUBTITLE_OF, TRANSCRIPT_OF and DERIVED_FROM.

Vectors attach to canonical content/assets via TPS_VECTOR; they do not replace hashes or identity.
