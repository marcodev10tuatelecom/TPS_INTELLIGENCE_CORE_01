# Advertising and Commercial Domain

Entities: ADVERTISER, CAMPAIGN, CREATIVE, COMMERCIAL, CONTRACT, INVENTORY_UNIT, PLACEMENT, PRODUCT, BRAND, AUDIENCE_SEGMENT.

Relations: SPONSORED_BY, TARGETS, AUTHORIZED_FOR, SCHEDULED_ON, COMPETES_WITH, PROMOTES, OWNED_BY.

Deterministic constraints include contract validity, territory, daypart, station/channel eligibility, maximum frequency, competitive separation, content restrictions and inventory availability.

Every commercial play event links placement, creative, campaign, channel, program/context and observed time for reconciliation and audit.
