# Retention, Rights and Compliance Architecture

Retention is data-class and legal/business-purpose driven. Canonical identity/history, contracts/rights, audience data, editorial evidence, audit and AI decisions may require different schedules.

Deletion requirements must reconcile privacy/legal obligations with audit/integrity. Where erasure is required, design pseudonymization/redaction/tombstoning without fabricating history.

Rights evaluation is a production safety function; source documents and effective windows must be traceable.
