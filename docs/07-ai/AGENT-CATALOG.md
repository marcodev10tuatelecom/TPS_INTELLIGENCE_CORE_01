# Governed Agent Catalog v0.01

## TPS_AI_PROGRAM_DIRECTOR
Recommends programs/content for schedule context. Advisory. Cannot authorize rights/schedule execution.

## TPS_AI_MUSIC_DIRECTOR
Ranks music candidates using program profile, recent history, audience, rights and vectors. Advisory.

## TPS_AI_NEWS_DIRECTOR
Summarizes/classifies verified editorial sources; cannot publish unverified claims automatically unless a future bounded workflow explicitly permits it.

## TPS_AI_COMMERCIAL_DIRECTOR
Ranks eligible campaigns/creatives after deterministic eligibility; cannot override contract/frequency/competitor rules.

## TPS_AI_AUDIENCE_ANALYST
Analyzes aggregates/affinity. No unauthorized personal-data expansion.

## TPS_AI_CONTENT_CURATOR
Finds related content, duplicates and metadata gaps.

## TPS_AI_RIGHTS_ANALYST
Explains rights evidence/conflicts. Does not create legal authorization.

## TPS_AI_COMPLIANCE
Detects policy anomalies and opens recommendations/alerts.

## TPS_AI_ARCHIVIST
Assists classification, lineage and retention suggestions.

## TPS_AI_SCHEDULER
Produces candidate schedules subject to policy engine.

## TPS_AI_NETWORK_OPERATOR
Advises on operational topology/incidents; cannot change infrastructure without separately authorized tool policy.

## TPS_AI_KNOWLEDGE_STEWARD
Detects duplicate entities, conflicting assertions, missing provenance and stale relations; proposes corrections for governed approval.
